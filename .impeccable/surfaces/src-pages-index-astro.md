# Surface brief: SHCHAKIM site (home first, all routes inherit)

Scope: whole marketing + booking site, home page leads. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Israeli couples 28–50 arriving from Instagram on a phone; reviewers on desktop. Action: book a night (/book wizard). Proof: the real crater, the real sky (live conditions, moon phases, sky map), honest prices.
Constraints: brief palette + fonts fixed; R1–R10 and booking keep working; reviewer feedback: photos must read as one real place, typography must be consistent.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the night map of Makhtesh Ramon: a topographic trail map where contours, spot heights and marked trails carry the page, and the lodge is a point on the rim. It refuses the dark full-bleed luxury-hotel photo page the beta was.
OWN-WORLD: Night ground (#0B0E1A) with sand (#D9A86C) contour lines and a faint 1-km map grid; Israeli trail blazes (starlight–ember–starlight stripes) mark actions, active states and stops; ember is reserved for trails and "now". Frank Ruhl Libre 500 sets every heading, Assistant 400/600 sets text and map labels with tabular numerals. Square 2px corners, hairline rules, legend boxes, survey triangles, scale bars. Every photo is a real Ramon photo under one night-to-sand duotone grade, captioned like a field plate. Suites are drawn as architectural sections in sand ink.
STORY: The visitor learns where this is (the crater at night), how dark it is (live sky data), picks a marked point (a suite), picks a night (moon), and books.
FIRST VIEWPORT: An elevation profile across the crater fills the bottom third: three ridge profiles with contour hatching, the sky above shifting from dusk to night as you scroll while the sun drops behind the rim. Headline at the inline-start top two thirds, Frank Ruhl 500 at ~5.5rem desktop, with the coordinates and elevation as a map label; the primary "להזמין לילה" button carries a trail blaze and sits under the lead. A scale bar and north arrow sit in the frame corners.
FORM: Topographic trail map of Makhtesh Ramon (my #1 of seven; chosen by the user over the assigned #5 observatory logbook). Seed key 50f7ffec. Raises: from the oscilloscope bench, every block sits on one measured map grid; from the risograph cover, a strict two-ink discipline (sand line + starlight text on night) with ember only for trails and "now".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
